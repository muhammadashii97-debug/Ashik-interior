## Facts used (from the brochures, not invented)
- Tiers: Smart Essentials (Sainik MR + HDHMR laminate), Signature Comfort — MOST CHOSEN
  (Sainik 710 BWP + HDHMR acrylic/glass, Hettich-level), Forma Luxe (Club Prime BWP + HDHMR PU/glass).
- 2BHK: ₹4,50,000 / ₹6,50,000 / ₹8,50,000 + 18% GST (kitchen ≤75 sq ft, 2 wardrobes 6x7, study, TV+panel, crockery)
- 3BHK: ₹6,00,000 / ₹8,00,000 / ₹10,00,000 + 18% GST (kitchen ≤80 sq ft, 3 wardrobes 6x7, study, TV+panel, crockery)
- Per sq ft from: Kitchen 2,200/3,200/4,200 · Wardrobe 2,100/3,400/4,400 · TV 1,600/1,900/2,500
  · Study 1,800/2,000/2,500 · Crockery 2,000/2,500/3,100 (+GST). Machine finish +₹50 / +₹100.
- 45-day target delivery*, 1-year workmanship warranty, 2D approval before fabrication, 6-step process.
- Excludes: countertop, sink/tap/tiles, appliances, plumbing/electrical, civil, false ceiling, loose furniture.
