# Forma Studio — how to film interiors (iPhone 17 / DJI gimbal)

Written from the problems found in Forma's own raw footage, Nov 2026.

## 1. Before you film (5 minutes of site prep)
- Remove: stickers and labels (basin, glass, appliances), boxes on top of units, bags,
  paint buckets, loose wires, water bottles, cups, clothes on beds.
- Lights: turn ON every light, including LED strips and backlights. Open curtains.
- Wipe glossy shutters and countertops (fingerprints show on acrylic).
- Open the doors and drawers you want to show, check the inside is tidy, then close them.

## 2. Camera settings

### iPhone 17
- Settings > Camera > Record Video: **4K at 30 fps** (use **4K 60 fps** for anything you
  want in slow motion).
- Settings > Camera: turn **Grid** and **Level** ON.
- Turn **HDR Video OFF** for consistent colour between clips.
- Lens: use **1x** for rooms, **2x** for close-ups. Avoid **0.5x** (ultra-wide) — it bends
  walls and cabinets, the "fisheye" look in the current footage.
- Before each shot, **tap and hold** on the cabinet to lock focus and exposure (AE/AF
  LOCK), then drag the sun icon down slightly if windows are too bright.
- Hold the phone **vertical** for reels.

### DJI gimbal (DJI Mimo app)
- Resolution **4K**, frame rate **30** (or **60** for slow motion).
- Aspect ratio **9:16** for reels. Do NOT use the 2.35:1 "cinematic" crop — the current
  DJI clips are 1920x816, which leaves only 816 px of width for a vertical reel and looks
  soft when enlarged.
- Gimbal mode **Tilt Locked** (keeps the horizon level). Never use **FPV** or **SpinShot**
  for interiors — those modes created the rotating / upside-down clips.
- Recentre the gimbal before every shot (double-tap the trigger).

## 3. How to move
- One movement per clip: **push in**, **slide sideways**, **tilt up**, or **stay still**.
- Move **slowly** — half your normal walking speed. Walk heel-to-toe, elbows bent.
- **Hold still for 3 seconds at the start and end** of every clip.
- Keep every clip **8–10 seconds** long (the current 4–5 s clips are too short to edit).
- Camera at **chest height**, pointing straight ahead (not looking down or up).
- Never spin the phone, and never tilt it sideways.

## 4. Shot list for every unit (kitchen, wardrobe, TV unit, vanity, showcase)
1. **Wide, closed** — straight-on, 4 seconds still, then a slow push in.
2. **Reveal** — the same position, a hand opens the doors / shutter / drawer slowly.
   Film it twice.
3. **Inside** — slow tilt or slide across shelves, drawers, organisers.
4. **3 close-ups (2x lens, 3 s each)** — handle, edge or finish, soft-close drawer or hinge.
5. **Context** — one wide shot of the whole room showing where the unit sits.

## 5. Light and glass
- Film in daylight with all lights on; avoid pointing straight into a bright window.
- For glass shutters and mirrors, stand at a slight angle so you are not in the reflection.

## 6. Upload
- Upload the original files (Photos > Share > Options > All Photos Data, or AirDrop / Drive
  "original quality"). WhatsApp compresses video — do not use it for raw footage.
